Privacy Policy

Chilenje Church of Christ Hymns

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how the Chilenje Church of Christ Hymns mobile app (“the app”) handles information. The app is published for Chilenje Church of Christ. The Android package name is com.chilenjecoc.

No account is required. The app does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or give your name, email address, or phone number.

Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information.

  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tools that track you.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect it.

Hymn text is stored in the app on your device so you can read and search it without creating an account. That content stays on your device.

Internet use

You can use the hymn books without an internet connection. If you tap the “Powered By Chilenje COC media Team” link, your browser opens codelabfirm.netlify.app. That website is separate from this app and has its own practices. The app itself does not receive information about you from that visit.

Children

The app is a hymn book and may be used by people of any age. It is not a social network. Because the app does not require an account and does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep a profile or a database of app users. Uninstalling the app removes the app and its hymn books from your device. There is no account to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated page will be posted at this same address with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the Chilenje Church of Christ media team through the website linked in the app: codelabfirm.netlify.app.
